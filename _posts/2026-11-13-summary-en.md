### [DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash Achieves Near-NVFP4 Quality on Eight V100 GPUs with 524K Context](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1xus81t/lawyer_with_too_many_gpus_part_5_deepseekv41flash/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A lawyer successfully optimized DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash to run a 524K context locally on eight V100 GPUs from 2018, achieving near-NVFP4 quality and high decode speeds. Using a custom &quot;Volta Max uPLIft&quot; method with custom kernels and requantization, the setup delivers 100+ tokens/second for single requests and approximately 160 tokens/second for two concurrent chats. This local implementation, publicly available on GitHub, now powers the author&\#x27;s legal application, replacing tasks previously handled by commercial models like Claude and Opus.

reddit · r/LocalLLaMA · /u/TumbleweedNew6515 · Nov 12, 23:31

**「Background」** DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash is a sparse mixture-of-experts large language model known for its efficiency, while Claude Opus is another advanced LLM recognized for strong performance in complex tasks. Optimizing large language models often involves techniques like custom kernels to enhance GPU performance.

**「Impact」** This achievement demonstrates that older, readily available V100 GPUs can be optimized to run large, high-performance language models like DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash with significant context and speed, enabling 100% local and private processing for demanding applications such as legal work, thereby offering a production-grade alternative to commercial cloud-based solutions.
