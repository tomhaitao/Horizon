### [AI Deciphers Kurt Gödel&\#x27;s Notebooks, Potentially Revealing Hundreds of Theorems](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Artificial intelligence has been used to decipher the previously unreadable notebooks of mathematician Kurt Gödel, which are said to contain hundreds of new, proved theorems. This application showcases a significant use of AI in historical and scientific discovery, offering potential new insights into the work of a foundational figure in logic and mathematics.

hackernews · bookofjoe · Nov 13, 17:30 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50486748)

**「Background」** Kurt Gödel was an Austrian logician whose 1931 incompleteness theorems profoundly influenced mathematics and the digital age. After his seminal work in the 1930s, Gödel published few papers, but maintained extensive philosophical notebooks, including his &quot;Max Phil&quot; series, which were written in Gabelsberger shorthand and have been the subject of transcription efforts. 

**「Impact」** The decipherment of Gödel&\#x27;s notebooks by AI could significantly advance understanding in mathematical logic by making available previously inaccessible insights and potentially hundreds of new theorems from a pivotal figure in the field.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters debated the likelihood of finding &quot;conclusive new theorems,&quot; with one user \(\[iltennico\]\) suggesting Gödel&\#x27;s perfectionism makes it improbable he would have overlooked such discoveries, though sharp insights are expected. Another user \(\[hobo123\]\) questioned the claim that only six people could read Gabelsberger shorthand, which Gödel used, based on personal experience.
